# OverSummoned brand icon mockups

Four options for a social profile picture, Gmail / Stripe avatar, and favicon. Every option uses the existing gold crown from the OverSummoned wordmark as the core mark. Nothing here has been published.

## How to use

- **Profile picture / Gmail / Stripe:** upload `option_N/profile_1024.png`. It is a square that is composed to look good when services crop it to a circle. `profile_circle_1024.png` is a preview of that crop, not the upload file.
- **Favicon:** use `option_N/favicon.ico` (16, 32, and 48 px in one file), or the individual `favicon_16.png`, `favicon_32.png`, and `favicon_48.png`. The `@8x` files are zoomed pixel previews for review only.
- **Compare them all:** see `option_sheet.png`.

## The options

1. **Summoning circle** — The gold crown over a glowing cyan, pink, and violet Aldermere summoning circle. Strongest as a circular avatar and as a tiny favicon.
2. **Collector card back** — The crown as the emblem on a dark purple card back, with a gold and pink rarity edge. Reads clearly as the card game.
3. **Hero from a card** — The crown as a crest over a card, with the legionary stepping out of a summoned portal. Tells the whole game story. The 16 and 32 px favicons drop the hero so the crown and card stay readable.
4. **Monogram O** — The official gold O from the OverSummoned wordmark, with the crown perched on it the same way it sits on the logotype.

Source marks (the official crown and the official O) are in `src/`. Rebuild with `python3 build_icons.py`.
