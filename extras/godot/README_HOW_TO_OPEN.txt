OverSummoned Desktop  —  Godot 4.3 (GL Compatibility)
=====================================================

1. Unzip OverSummonedDesktop_Godot4.zip to a folder on your PC.
2. Open Godot 4.3 (or later 4.x) from Steam or the Godot Engine, click Import,
   and choose the project.godot file in this folder.
3. Press Play (F5). The summoner sits in a transparent always-on-top window.

Quick test: click the pedestal or press keys while the window is focused to
charge the rift. Press F8 or open the ✦ menu → Drops → "Test Summon Now!" to
watch an item pop out of the circle and fly into your inventory. (On Windows,
turn on "Count clicks & keys anywhere" in Settings to count desktop-wide.)
