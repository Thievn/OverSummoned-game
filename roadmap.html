What Smudge is summoning next

The ledger is not finished and neither is the game. Here is what has just arrived, what is being worked on now, and what is further down the road. No dates, because Smudge cannot be trusted with a calendar.

Just arrivedBeing builtAfter thatOn the horizon
1

Just arrived

Stamped and in the game

Already playable. If you have not looked lately, start here.

The cityFriends, gifts, a mailbox, and a look at anyone's ledger and crew.
GuildsUp to thirty players, one shared goal a week, crates and shards when you hit it.
The marketPut spare gear and treasure on your stall and name your price.
Crew and expeditionsThree arrivals stand behind Smudge. Everyone else goes exploring and brings things home.
Eight gear slots and era setsEvery hero has three pieces only they can wear, plus sets from their corner of history.
RebirthHit level 99, start the skill again, keep a permanent bonus. Ten times over.
2

Being built

Ink still drying

On the desk right now. These are next into the game.

A fair ledgerEvery haul, drop and summon rolled by the server, so the rankings and the market mean something.
Mastery for every treasureThe more often you find a thing, the better you get at finding it.
The town crierA feed of what happened while you were away: sales, gifts, guild news, who passed you.
Live guild roomChat that updates as people talk, with mute and report.
3

After that

Pencilled in

Planned and designed, waiting their turn. This is where the game gets bigger.

New arrivals every month RegularEach new hero brings their own three pieces of gear, a five-piece era set for everyone, new lines for the crew, and new treasure for the cabinet.
Guild raidsBosses too big for three. The whole guild chips in over a week and splits the haul.
SeasonsRankings reset on a schedule. Finish high for a card frame and a title you keep.
New places and new tradesMore corners of history to work in, and more skills for Smudge to be bad at first.
EventsShort, strange weeks with their own loot table. Miss one and it comes round again.
Daily stampStamp the ledger each day for a growing reward. Miss a day and you keep your place.
Titles and featsHundreds of small goals with a title to wear in the city for each.
The shop Looks onlyOutfits for Smudge, card frames, new looks for the cellar, and a supporter badge. Nothing in the shop makes a card stronger.
