The Story

OverSummoned update log

Everything new in the city, newest first.

November 23, 2026

The long road

  • Skills take much longer to master now. Every level you already earned stays yours.
  • Hustles open up as your Hustle level grows. Bigger jobs pay more and teach you more.
  • Healing draughts and guard oil now need ingredients from other skills.
  • Violet gems are a rare find in the Starfall crater now.
  • Card shards stay with you for the circle instead of going to the stall.
November 23, 2026

Little touches around the city

  • Some heroes have a little more life in them.
November 23, 2026

Faces front and center

  • Profile pictures are framed so every face sits right in the middle.
  • Your collection now shows the heroes you own first, with their names tucked under the picture.
  • Names can include an apostrophe now, like O'Brien.
  • Want OverSummoned on your home screen? Look for the Get the app card on your Account page.
  • Heroes in the city banner wander off and stroll back in, so your crowd keeps mixing.
November 23, 2026

The city comes alive

  • The banner on your city page now shows ten streets from your heroes' home times, and it drifts from one to the next.
  • Every hero you've collected strolls along the street, so your crowd grows as your collection does.
  • Keep an eye out: now and then two of your heroes stop and have a little chat.
  • Calmer screens keeps everyone standing still if you'd rather the city rest.
November 23, 2026

Your own corner of the city

  • Tap your picture at the top right to open your new Account page.
  • Pick a new name and see right away if it's free.
  • Every hero you collect unlocks a new profile picture.
  • See everything you own at a glance, and turn motion, update notes and the install button on or off.
  • The featured card turns over to show its own back, and the city banner keeps every hero in view.
November 23, 2026

Take OverSummoned with you

  • You can now install OverSummoned on your phone or computer and open it from its own icon.
  • On iPhone and iPad, a quick guide shows you how to add it to your Home Screen.
  • Opening the app plays a short summoning circle. Tap anywhere to skip it.
  • If your signal drops, a friendly page waits with you and reopens the city once you're back.
  • Card fronts are now still pictures, gear on the back stays put, and the backs are brighter.
November 23, 2026

What's new, right in the game

  • A bar at the top of the game now tells you when something new has landed. Tap it to read what changed.
  • Every update is written down in the new update log, so you can always look back.
November 23, 2026

Your squad, in the room

  • The delve room now shows the heroes you actually sent down.
  • The sigil rite pays out at a steady pace, and your progress toward the next sigil is kept between visits.
November 23, 2026

Collections and new gear sets

  • New Collections page. Everything you find lights up, and anything still out there stays gray.
  • New gear sets to find, one for every hero. Wear all three pieces on the right hero for a bonus.
  • Skills keep every bit of progress between visits, and Vigor and Daring refill properly while you are away.
  • Hustles can bring back a little extra now and then. Some jobs can also go wrong.
November 22, 2026

Gear and real fights

  • Heroes now wear gear. Weapons, rings, trinkets and armor all change how a fight goes.
  • Flip a card to see what your hero is wearing and swap things in from your bank.
  • Delve fights now play out swing by swing, and potions on the belt really get used.
  • More loot added to the delves.
November 22, 2026

A warmer welcome

  • A fresh front page with the heroes, screenshots and the story of Aldermere.
  • Signing in is quicker, and the game remembers you on this device.
November 22, 2026

OverSummoned opens its doors

  • The river city of Aldermere is open. Summon heroes, put them to work, and come back to see what they found.
  • Seven skills to train, delves to send your squad on, and a market where prices move.
