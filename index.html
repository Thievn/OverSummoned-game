A desktop pet with a filing problem

He stamped the wrong name. Now history keeps showing up.

Three hundred years ago a clerk's apprentice wrote one name in the wrong book. He has been summoning the wrong people ever since, one bewildered stranger at a time. The apprentice is an otter called Smudge. He lives on your taskbar now, and every key you press makes it worse.

How it gets out of hand

You were only answering email.

1

You type. He works.

Emails, essays, arguments with strangers. Smudge reads none of it. He feels the keys and gets on with the job: stamping the ledger, fishing the Tiber, cracking a safe that is not his.

2

Somebody falls out of history.

Fill the circle and Smudge summons a stranger. Rarely the one he meant. A Roman legionary. A pirate afraid of seagulls. A corgi who conquered Europe and still cannot reach the top shelf. They all need somewhere to stand.

3

Their things follow them.

Whatever each arrival was carrying scatters through time and washes up where Smudge works. Find the sword, the hat, the suspiciously warm lantern, and put them back in the right paws.

The arrivals

A crowd of strangers, and Smudge is not finished. None of them asked to be here.

Each one is a card. Turn it over for the story, the numbers, and the three things they lost on the way. Here are five. The rest you will have to meet yourself, and Smudge is nowhere near finished: every time he sneezes near the ledger, somebody new falls out.

The rest are waiting in the margins, and more are on the way.

Meet all the arrivals

Lost property

Every item has a past. Most of them are embarrassing.

Gear makes whoever holds it stronger, faster or luckier, and it works half again as well in the paws it was made for. Every label tells you where it turns up, so you always know what you are hunting. This is not the whole list. It is not even close. The otter keeps summoning, and every new arrival drops three more things somewhere in time.

Where he works

Eight corners of history, and one cellar he calls home.

A Roman pier. A Viking ice hole. A Venetian vault. An office supply closet that has defeated better thieves. Each place has its own trade and its own tide of lost things.

Questions

Everything people ask before they start.

What is OverSummoned?

OverSummoned is a free desktop pet and idle game. Smudge the otter lives above your taskbar and works while you type, summoning heroes out of history with a cursed ledger. The same heroes can be put to work in your browser.

Can I play it now?

Not openly yet. OverSummoned is in closed beta. Join the beta on the home page and you will get an email when a place opens for you.

How does the desktop pet work?

Smudge reacts to your typing. Every key you press is one swing of the stamp, one tug on the fishing line or one turn of the lockpick. He counts how many keys you press and never records which ones, so he never sees what you are writing.

Is OverSummoned free?

Yes. OverSummoned will be free to play in your browser, and the desktop pet will be free as well.

Do I need to download anything?

No. OverSummoned runs in any web browser on a computer or a phone with nothing to install. The desktop pet is an optional free download, and it is coming to Steam.

Is it an idle game?

Yes. Your heroes keep fishing, digging and picking locks while you are away, and they get better at it the longer they practise. Everything they did still counts when you come back.

How many heroes are there?

Dozens so far, and Smudge keeps misfiring, so nobody is keeping an exact count. Each arrival is a collectible card with its own story, stats and three lost items.

Where does Smudge work?

Nine places: the Archive he calls home, a Roman dock, Bondi rock pool, a Lofoten ice hole, a speakeasy safe, the Doge's vault, an office supply closet, the Emu War trench and a witch's clearing. Each has its own trade and its own lost things.

Can I change how Smudge talks?

Yes. Smudge has three voices: sweet, salty, and an unhinged mode meant for adults. You can change it whenever he gets to be too much.

Is OverSummoned on Steam?

The desktop pet is coming to Steam. The browser game is in closed beta: sign up at oversummoned.com and Smudge sends your invite.
