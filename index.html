A text crime RPG. You hit the street as a drifter with nothing worth taking. The jobs you repeat start to show. Keep at a thing long enough and the people who do it for a living notice, and make you an offer.

02

On the street

The Den is a building. The life is everything around it. You can steal, drive, stitch, lie, or stand where you're told. There is no costume waiting on a menu.

01

Crimes

A pocket on the late bus. A padlock on a dock shed. Small at first. Do it the same way twice and the people who live that way start to recognize the hand.

02

Crews and heists

Some scores need a driver, a pair of hands, and someone mean enough to watch the door. You write the split before anyone goes in. Arguments happen after, if you're lucky.

03

Black market

Papers, medicine, a piece with the numbers gone, a car that won't show on a list. When the precinct gets bored, the prices get brave.

04

Jail and breakouts

Slip and you sit. Days move whether you do or not. A friend outside can shorten it. A clever idea can shorten it faster, or buy you a longer stay.

05

Property and turf

A room over a closed shop. Then the shop. Then the block that goes quiet when you come around the corner. Call it rent if you need a cleaner word.

06

Faction wars

Older outfits already cut the map up. They don't paint the lines. You find one when a job on the wrong street ends with a simple question: who sent you?

03

Five parts of town

Learn them before they learn you. The rest is miles of street that will pick up your name if you give it a reason.
