How it works

Three steps.

1

Summon heroes.

Every summon is a new character card: a Roman soldier, a pirate, a corgi who conquered Europe.

2

Put them to work.

Send heroes fishing, digging and safecracking. They earn loot even when the game is closed.

3

Gear up and fight.

Equip what they find, train them, and take on bosses.

What you can do

Plenty to keep them busy.

Jobs

Send Smudge to fish, dig and pick locks, with heroes behind him to make him faster and luckier. He keeps going while you are away.

Raids

Pick three heroes and take on a boss. Win and you bring home the best loot that place has.

Workshop and Training

Turn spare loot into tonics and better gear. Spend energy to raise a hero's stats.

Desktop pet

Optional: a small Smudge on your taskbar who reacts when you type. He counts key presses and never sees what you type.

Questions

Before you sign up

Is it free?

Yes. No ads. Nothing you can buy makes you stronger.

What do I need?

A browser. Works on phone and computer.

Is the beta open?

It is invite-only, and everyone who signs up gets an invite right away.

Does the desktop pet read my typing?

No. It only counts key presses.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, to your account, across devices.
