How it works

You type. Smudge slaps. History happens.

  1. 1

    Work, game, chat

    Smudge lives on your taskbar. Every key press is a slap on the ledger. He only counts presses, never what you type.

  2. 2

    The circle fills

    Ink pools in the summoning circle. When it overflows, someone tumbles out: a legionary, a flapper, a confused office worker.

  3. 3

    Put them to work

    Open the City on your phone. Mine, fish, cook, send a squad into a delve, pull a hustle. They keep working while you don't.

Questions

Good questions, short answers.

What is OverSummoned?

An idle game in two halves. A desktop companion on Steam, where Smudge the otter summons people from history while you type, and the City of Aldermere in your browser, where you put them to work.

Does it read what I type?

No. The desktop app counts key presses, the same way a step counter counts steps. It never sees which keys, words or passwords.

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free. Optional cosmetics are sold through Steam, and nothing you can buy is random or makes you stronger.

Do I need the Steam app to play on my phone?

No. Open oversummoned.com on your phone and sign in with Steam. It installs like an app from your browser.

Who are the heroes?

Parody heroes pulled out of their own time: a Roman legionary, a plague doctor from 1348, an Ice Age hunter, a Chicago gangster, a Victorian engineer, a Hollywood leading man, and some very confused folks from today.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Join a crew for a shared chat, a shared vault and a weekly goal that everyone's typing fills.

When does it launch?

The City is being rebuilt now and the desktop app is next. Leave your email and you'll hear the moment the doors open.
