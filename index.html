A desktop pet with a filing problem

He stamped the wrong name. Now history keeps showing up.

Three hundred years ago a clerk's apprentice wrote one name in the wrong book. The book has been correcting the mistake ever since, one bewildered stranger at a time. The apprentice is an otter called Smudge. He lives on your taskbar now, and every key you press makes it worse.

How it gets out of hand

You were only answering email.

1

You type. He works.

Emails, essays, arguments with strangers. Smudge reads none of it. He feels the keys and gets on with the job: stamping the ledger, fishing the Tiber, cracking a safe that is not his.

2

Somebody falls out of history.

Fill the circle and the book coughs up a stranger. A Roman legionary. A pirate afraid of seagulls. A corgi who conquered Europe and still cannot reach the top shelf. They all need somewhere to stand.

3

Their things follow them.

Whatever each arrival was carrying scatters through time and washes up where Smudge works. Find the sword, the hat, the suspiciously warm lantern, and put them back in the right paws.

The arrivals

Twenty-six strangers and counting. None of them asked to be here.

Each one is a card. Turn it over for the story, the numbers, and the three things they lost on the way. Here are five. The rest you will have to meet yourself, and the book is nowhere near finished: every time Smudge sneezes near the ledger, somebody new falls out.

Twenty-one more are waiting in the margins. More are on the way.

Lost property

Every item has a past. Most of them are embarrassing.

Gear makes whoever holds it stronger, faster or luckier, and it works half again as well in the paws it was made for. Every label tells you where it turns up, so you always know what you are hunting. This is not the whole list. It is not even close. The otter keeps summoning, and every new arrival drops three more things somewhere in time.

Where he works

Eight corners of history, and one cellar he calls home.

A Roman pier. A Viking ice hole. A Venetian vault. An office supply closet that has defeated better thieves. Each place has its own trade and its own tide of lost things.

What you do with them

Somebody has to keep twenty-six strangers busy.

Set it up in a minute on your phone, then leave them to it. They keep working while you sleep, and they are always a little better at it when you come back.

Jobs

Send a Viking fishing and a fox safecracking. Speed sets the pace, Luck picks the haul, and practice turns into mastery.

Workshop

Boil spare treasure into tonics. Melt duplicate gear into something sharper.

Training

Energy trickles back all day. Spend it turning an office sloth into a slightly tougher office sloth.

Raids

Three arrivals against the Tiber Thing, the Doge's Nephew, or Brenda, back from leave. Winners take the best loot there is.

The otter talks

Pick how much attitude you can take.

Smudge has an opinion on everything he finds and everyone he summons. Choose his mood. Change it the moment he gets too much.
