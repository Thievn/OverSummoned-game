City Hub

One screen for the whole evening.

The hub is where you start. You can see who is still at the rock, how far the delve has gone, what the city paper is shouting about, and how close your crew is to the week's goal. Will Slapton is usually on the right, waiting to be pointed at something loud.

Skills

Seven crafts that keep going after you leave.

Mining, woodcutting, fishing, cooking, smithing, alchemy, and summoning. Put a hero on a shift and they stay with it. If you are gone for a day, the work still counts when you walk back in.

Delve

Send them down, then stay for the fight.

You pick how long they are gone, from a short walk to most of a night. The battle plays in front of you. When it turns ugly you get to kick the pot, raise the shields, or stop and eat.

Cards

A face on the front, a life on the back.

Collect the hero, then turn the card over. The back holds what they wear and the skill they bring. Extra copies make the card stronger, and better gear makes them hit harder.

The nine

Nine heroes, and the row is only the start.

They drift past on their own. Grab the row to look closer, tap a card to turn it, and touch any piece of gear to see what it does.

Market

The price moves because people are buying.

Moonsilver, stew, gems, and cards change hands all day. Buy them where a waystone makes them cheap, and sell them in Aldermere when the city wants them.

Crews

A crew that shows up when it gets dark.

You get a chat, a shared vault, and a goal for the week. At night the squad can sign up for the big fight. The Crew Cup is just the city keeping score, out loud.

Questions

Good questions, short answers.

What is OverSummoned?

OverSummoned is a pixel idle RPG you play in your browser. You summon heroes from different eras of history, put them to work in the river city of Aldermere, and send them on delves for loot.

Is OverSummoned an idle game?

Yes. Your heroes keep mining, fishing, cooking and fighting while you are away. When you come back, everything they did still counts.

Do I need to download anything?

No. OverSummoned runs in your web browser on a computer or a phone. There is nothing to install.

Is OverSummoned free?

Yes. OverSummoned is a free online RPG you can play right in your browser.

Who are the heroes?

They are parody heroes pulled out of their own time. There is a Roman legionary, a plague doctor from 1348, an Ice Age hunter, a Chicago gangster from 1927, a Victorian engineer, a Hollywood leading man, and a few folks from the modern day. Nine are in the city right now, with more on the way.

How do loot and gear work?

Heroes bring back gear from their delves, like weapons, trinkets, rings and potions for their belt. Every item has stats such as Attack, Defense, Health and Speed, and those numbers count in every fight.

What skills can my heroes learn?

There are seven: mining, woodcutting, fishing, cooking, smithing, alchemy and summoning. Each hero is better at some of them, so who you send where matters.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Join a crew to get a shared chat, a shared vault and a goal for the week. At night your squad can sign up for the big fight together.
