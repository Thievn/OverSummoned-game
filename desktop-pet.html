A desktop pet that actually has a job

Most desktop pets wander about and knock things over. Smudge works. Every key you press is one swing of his stamp — and sooner or later somebody falls out of history because of it.

What it actually does

Smudge sits in a small window above your taskbar while you get on with whatever you were doing. He is not a wallpaper and not a browser tab. He reacts in real time: type and he stamps, stop and he dozes off, come back and he wakes up visibly annoyed about it.

Half an hour of ordinary typing and a crate drops out of a hole in time. The strangers he summons go on to fish, dig and pick locks in nine places scattered across history, and they carry on while you sleep.

He counts your keys. He never reads them.

This is the part worth being plain about, because "reacts to typing" and "keylogger" sound uncomfortably similar.

The pet needs to know that a key went down. It does not need to know which one, so it does not look. No characters are captured, nothing is stored, nothing is transmitted, and the contents of your windows are never touched. What reaches the server is a count of presses and the progress it earned. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

If you liked the others

If you have ever kept a Bongo Cat on your screen, let a Desktop Goose ruin an afternoon, or grown up with a Shimeji wandering across your windows, you already know the appeal: something alive in the corner of a working day.

OverSummoned keeps that and adds a reason to leave it running. The others are a toy you watch. This one quietly builds you a collection of thirty-six strangers out of history while you answer email — and you can pick the whole thing up later in your browser, on the same account.

Questions

What is a desktop pet?

A desktop pet is a small character that lives on your screen outside any window, usually near the taskbar, and reacts to what you do. Unlike a game you open and close, a desktop pet is ambient: it is simply there while you work.

Is OverSummoned's desktop pet free?

Yes. The desktop pet is free, with no ads and nothing to buy to use it. The browser game is free as well.

Does the desktop pet record what I type?

No. It counts how many keys you press so Smudge knows when to swing the stamp. It never records which keys, never stores the characters you type, and never reads the contents of any window. No keystroke content leaves your computer, because none is captured.

Will it slow my computer down?

It is a small window with a single animated character. It is built to sit quietly in the background while you work and does not run the browser game alongside it.

What platforms does it run on?

The desktop pet is being built for Windows and is coming to Steam. The browser game already runs on any modern browser, on a computer or a phone, with nothing to install.

Can I use it without the desktop app?

Yes. Everything the pet does has a home in the browser game at oversummoned.com, and the two share one account once you link them.
