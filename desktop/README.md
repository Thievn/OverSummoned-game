# OverSummoned Desk (sample)

Smudge, the hooded otter apprentice, sits above your taskbar. Typing powers whatever he is doing.

## Run it

1. Open Godot 4 (4.3 or newer).
2. **Import** → pick `desktop/project.godot` → **Import & Edit**.
3. Press **F5** (or the play button). The first open takes a few seconds while Godot imports the art.

## Play

- **Type** in Smudge's window, or click the scene. Each key press powers the current location.
- **Click Smudge** to open the menu: Places, Roster, Bag, Smudge (voice + your own AI key), Settings.
- **Right-drag** to move the window. **Esc** closes the menu.
- Settings → **Test mode** makes everything 10x faster. **Demo typing** types for you so you can just watch.

## Locations

| Place | Skill | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Archive | Summoning | The circle fills. Full circle = a hero card from history |
| Roman Bathhouse | Fishing | A bite every ~160 key presses. Roman loot and fish |
| Speakeasy Safe | Lockpicking | 5 pins, ~70 presses each. Cracked safe = 2-3 items + coins |

Skills level 1–99 on a Melvor-style curve. Higher levels are a little faster and luckier.

## Sample limits

- Keys are counted only while Smudge's window is focused. Counting presses in other apps (never which keys) needs a small native add-on that comes next.
- Progress is saved on this PC only (`user://oversummoned_desk.cfg`, encrypted). Steam and web sync come later.
