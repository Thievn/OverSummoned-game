Gear upgrades

Spare parts from the bench and some gold. Each level adds 15% to its numbers, up to level 5.

Training

Energy comes back on its own, one point every three minutes. Ten of it buys one point in whatever you pick. Higher card levels can be trained further.

Raids

Three arrivals against something that should have been left alone. Attack does the damage, Defense and Health keep them standing, Speed strikes first and Luck lands the big hits. Winning brings home the best things a place has.

Desktop pet

Smudge can sit on your desktop and work while you type, on this same ledger. Two ways to link it, both under the pet’s Settings: press Link in browser there and say Yes here, or get a code here and type it in. Once per computer: a second PC links again. Every code is random, works one time and runs out after ten minutes. When the game reaches Steam you will not need a code at all.
