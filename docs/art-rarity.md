# Art budget by rarity (new cards only)

When we make a new hero card, rarity sets how much motion it gets. Existing heroes keep the kits they already have. Card fronts stay still.

| Rarity | Typical PixelLab generations | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 2–4 | A still portrait and a simple walk, or just the portrait |
| Uncommon | 4–6 | Walk, maybe one short idle |
| Rare | 6–9 | Walk plus a short action or two |
| Epic | 8–12 | Walk and a fuller action set |
| Legendary | 10–16 | Walk, idle, and a few actions |
| Absurd and above | 12–20 | The full set, still with a still front |

Spend at the low end when the art is already good, and only go higher if a cycle really needs another pass. Do not strip motion from cards that are already in the game to "make them match."
