# A "How the ledger works" page for players

I offered Dean a lore page called "How the Ledger Works" earlier. He has not answered yet, so this is still just an offer.

`docs/lore-brief.md` already lists it as the first piece of lore to write ("How the ledger works", one page, "the load-bearing one"), so I am not writing the lore itself here. That job is for whoever Dean hands it to. This file only says what I think the page should do.

## What I would want from it

- Say the rule plainly: the ledger summons from the record, not from history. Anything that was written down, sung about, gossiped about or believed.
- Use the cards the game already has as the proof, exactly as the brief's table does. Cleocatra is a real person, bent. Gaius Marcellus is a nobody from an era. Florida Man is a legend. Gorm the Mighty is a toy from a 1983 aisle. Captain Stella Comet is a future that never happened. Zorp says "Beyond" and will not elaborate. Players who own those cards will see the rule at work straight away.
- Connect it to the two forms in `docs/direction-cards.md`: the animal is how they were remembered, the human is how they were recorded.
- Keep Smudge out of the explanation. The brief says the lore is the straight man and Smudge does the jokes.

## What I have deliberately left out

"The Order." I used that word in a chat with Dean, for a group that Tobias answered to and whose clerk writes down the names. It is my invention, it is not in the game, and `docs/lore-brief.md` is right to call it not canon. I am not proposing it here. If Dean ever wants a "who built the Archive" answer, it is one possible answer among others, to be decided on purpose.
