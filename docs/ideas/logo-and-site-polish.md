# Logo and site polish

This is an idea only. I have not touched the site, the repo's site files or any deploy. The mockups are images on my side.

## What Dean said

The site feels plain. He wants it to feel fuller without a drastic change. He also wants a more interesting logo that is themed to the site.

## Five logo concepts

I made five, each on dark and light backgrounds, with a favicon and an app icon.

1. **Inkwell Ledger.** A ledger book emblem with a teal ink blot and a gold quill, next to the wordmark. Ink drips run off the bottom of the letters. Straight from the world: the ledger, and ink being the thing that matters.
2. **Summoning Seal.** A round badge with a gold rune ring, a teal summoning star and an open book, over a gold wordmark and a thin divider.
3. **Smudge Peek.** Smudge peeks over a navy and gold nameplate with the wordmark on it, ink dripping off the bottom edge. The icon is Smudge over a gold S.
4. **Foil Card Crest.** A real foil card from the new frames, with a teal ink S inside a gold ring, and a human-form card tucked behind it.
5. **Engraved Gold.** A gold foil serif wordmark with a small sparkle and a teal gem. The icon is a gold OS monogram.

## What I would pick

**Inkwell Ledger for the main logo and Smudge Peek for the personality.** Use the ledger wordmark in the header and as the favicon, and use the Smudge peek as a recurring small touch around the site.

My reasoning: the ledger and the ink are the story of the game (`docs/lore-brief.md` calls the ledger the load-bearing idea), and the teal ink drip uses the colour the site already has. The Smudge peek is the friendliest of the five and he is already the face of the game. The Summoning Seal is pretty but looks like any fantasy game. The Foil Card Crest is a good idea for a Steam capsule later but too busy as a small icon. Engraved Gold looks expensive but has no connection to anything the game says.

## Making the site fuller, not different

Same colours (teal, gold, navy) and the same Lilita One headings. Add:

- **Ink dividers** between sections, in place of plain lines. A thin ink swipe with a drip.
- **Smudge corner peeks.** A small Smudge peering around the edge of a section or a card, a few places only, so it stays a surprise.
- **A card fan** in the hero: four or five real cards spread like a hand, using the new frame template.
- **A rarity showcase** strip: one card per rarity in a row, with the odds under each. It is the best way to say "seven rarities" and the odds are already public.
- A little more texture in the backgrounds (a soft parchment or ink grain), not a new look.

## Smudge notes

Smudge peeks and nothing else. He should not have speech bubbles on the marketing site that say new things. If any line goes next to him, it should come from `data/voice.json`. He does not use emoji, so none near him either.

## What I would need before doing it

Dean's choice of logo and a yes before anything touches the live site. The quick part is the logo, favicon, ink dividers and Smudge peeks. The card fan and the rarity showcase depend on the frame template in `card-frame-template.md` being settled.
