## 1. Doug from Accounts
*Capybara · Expense Auditor · Today · Common*

Doug has rejected your expenses. Not yours in particular: everyone's, since 2019. He was summoned halfway through a spreadsheet and has asked twice whether the ledger has been reconciled. It has not. He is fine with that. He is fine with everything. That is the unsettling part.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "A capybara in a lanyard! He looks so calm!" · salty: "Doug. Accounts. He will need a receipt for that." · unhinged: "It's Doug from fucking Accounts. Calmest bastard alive. He's already denied your lunch."

**Three lost items:** Red Pen of Rejection (*Three strokes and the words 'see me'.*) · Short-Sleeve Shirt, Tie Optional (*Breathable. Unremarkable. Proof against small talk.*) · Lanyard With Nine Keycards (*Only one of them opens anything. He knows which.*)

**Set, "Month-End":** Reading Glasses on a Cord, Fleece Vest, Company Logo, Orthopaedic Loafers

**Says:** I'll need a receipt for that fish. / That's coming out of petty cash. / No rush. There is never a rush. / Has anyone reconciled this ledger? / I'm not stressed. I have never been stressed.

**True fact:** The capybara is the largest rodent alive, and can stay underwater for up to five minutes.

## 2. Bjorn the Twice-Dead
*Walrus · Ice Fisherman · Norway, 870 · Common*

Bjorn has fallen through the ice twice and been declared dead both times. Both times he walked back into the village in time for supper. The gods keep sending him home and he has stopped asking why. He fishes. He waits. He is owed two funeral feasts and intends to collect.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "A big walrus with a fishing pole! So whiskery!" · salty: "Bjorn. Died twice. Bad at it." · unhinged: "Bjorn drowned twice and Valhalla sent the fat bastard back both times. Even the gods didn't want him."

**Three lost items:** Whalebone Ice Spear (*For the fish. And, once, for a very surprised bear.*) · Sealskin Coat, Still Damp (*It has never fully dried. Neither has he.*) · His Own Funeral Coin (*They left it on his eyes. He kept it. Seemed fair.*)

**Set, "Thin Ice":** Frost-Rimmed Fur Hood, Twice-Mended Fishing Smock, Hobnailed Ice Boots

**Says:** The ice is fine. Probably. / I have been colder. I have been dead. / Patience. The fish is also waiting. / Third time, I am told, is the charm. / Somebody owes me a funeral feast.

**True fact:** A walrus's tusks are teeth that never stop growing, and it uses them to haul itself out onto the ice.

## 3. Mona Lisard
*Chameleon · Portrait Painter · Venice, 1610 · Uncommon*

The most sought-after portrait painter in Venice, and no two clients agree on what she looks like. She turns the colour of whatever she stands beside, which is usually your valuables. Her sitters get a lovely painting. Later, they notice the room is emptier.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "A lizard with a paintbrush! She changed colour!" · salty: "Mona Lisard. Paints you. Then blends in with your silverware." · unhinged: "Mona'll paint your portrait and rob you blind in the same sitting, the sneaky little shit. Count the spoons."

**Three lost items:** Palette Knife, Sharpened (*For oils. Mostly.*) · Paint-Stained Smock (*Every colour she has ever been.*) · The Unfinished Smile (*A small canvas. She says it is done. Everyone else has questions.*)

**Set, "Sfumato":** Floppy Velvet Beret, Master's Apron, Soft Studio Slippers

**Says:** Hold still. No, stiller. / You never saw me. Technically true. / I'm matching the wall. Give me a moment. / That's not my colour. Now it is. / The eyes follow you. So do I.

**True fact:** A chameleon's eyes move separately, so it can look in two directions at once.

## 4. Dusty Belle
*Echidna · Gold Prospector · Western Australia, 1932 · Uncommon*

Dusty has been digging the same claim for eleven years and has found, in total, one nugget and a great many opinions. She watched the army lose a war to some birds from her front step and did not get up. She has a pickaxe, a grudge, and no intention of sharing either.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "A spiky digger with a pickaxe! Careful, pointy!" · salty: "Dusty Belle. Eleven years, one nugget, no patience." · unhinged: "Dusty's been digging that hole eleven fucking years. Do not ask her how it's going."

**Three lost items:** Old Faithful (*A pickaxe. Named after the only thing that never let her down.*) · Canvas Dungarees, Patched (*More patch than dungaree by now.*) · The One Nugget (*She will not tell you where. She will not tell you how big.*)

**Set, "Claim Jumper":** Sweat-Stained Slouch Hat, Prospector's Swag Vest, Steel-Toed Digging Boots

**Says:** Mine. All of it. Mine. / Dig your own hole. / I've seen better dirt. / Don't touch the nugget. / Eleven years. Don't.

**True fact:** Echidnas and the platypus are the only mammals that lay eggs.

## 5. Inspector Hootsworth
*Tawny Owl · Consulting Detective · London, 1888 · Rare*

The Inspector solves every case the same way: she stares at the suspect, in silence, without blinking, until they confess. It has never taken longer than four minutes. She can turn her head to look directly behind her and does so at dinner parties, which is why she is no longer invited.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "An owl detective! She has a tiny hat!" · salty: "Inspector Hootsworth. She already knows what you did." · unhinged: "Hootsworth just stares at you till you fucking confess. I told her about the biscuits. I told her EVERYTHING."

**Three lost items:** Magnifying Glass, Large (*She does not need it. It is for your benefit.*) · Inverness Cape (*Wool. Rainproof. Conceals a remarkable wingspan.*) · Notebook of Things You Said (*Dated. Cross-referenced. Underlined twice.*)

**Set, "Scotland Yard":** Deerstalker, Not in the Books, Tweed Waistcoat with Fob Chain, Silent-Soled Boots

**Says:** Who? I already know who. / Interesting. Do go on. / You blinked. Noted. / The fish did it. / I never sleep. I merely close one case.

**True fact:** An owl cannot move its eyes in their sockets, so it turns its head instead, up to 270 degrees.

## 6. Agent Sterling Stoat
*Stoat · Gentleman Spy · Chicago, 1927 · Rare*

Nobody knows who Sterling works for, including, by now, Sterling. He has infiltrated every speakeasy in Chicago and filed a detailed report on the cocktails. His white dinner jacket has never had a mark on it, which in his line of work is either great skill or a very bad sign.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "A fancy weasel in a white suit! So smooth!" · salty: "Agent Stoat. Licensed to mingle." · unhinged: "Slick little bastard's been 'undercover' in a bar for six years. On expenses. Doug is furious."

**Three lost items:** Cocktail Stick, Weighted (*Olive optional. Dangerous either way.*) · White Dinner Jacket (*Spotless. He will not say how.*) · Cufflink Camera (*Forty exposures. Thirty-eight are of canapes.*)

**Set, "Top Secret":** Low-Brimmed Fedora, Hidden-Pocket Cummerbund, Patent Leather Sneaking Shoes

**Says:** I was never here. / Shaken. I insist. / This message will not self-destruct. Budget cuts. / Trust no one. Especially me. / The password is 'password'. I despair.

**True fact:** In cold places a stoat's coat turns pure white for winter. The white fur is called ermine.

## 7. Dolly LaRue
*Fallow Deer · Canteen Sweetheart · Sydney, 1942 · Rare*

Dolly sang at the servicemen's canteen every night of the war, and every man in the room was certain she was singing to him. She was singing to the back wall, where the clock was. She has had forty-one proposals, kept all the rings, and now runs a very successful jeweller's.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "A singing deer! She has a flower in her hair!" · salty: "Dolly LaRue. Forty-one proposals. Kept every ring." · unhinged: "Dolly'll wink at you, take your ring and your whole damn paycheck, and you'll thank her. I did."

**Three lost items:** Chrome Stage Microphone (*Heavy base. Excellent swing.*) · Polka-Dot Halter Dress (*Rationed fabric. Unrationed effect.*) · Box of Forty-One Rings (*She remembers every name. That is the cruel part.*)

**Set, "Sweetheart":** Victory Rolls and a Hibiscus, Sailor-Collar Jacket, Peep-Toe Dancing Heels

**Says:** This one's for you, sugar. / Oh, you're sweet. Next. / Keep the rings coming, soldier. / I only sing for the clock, darling. / Smile. You're on shore leave.

**True fact:** Reindeer are the only deer in which the females grow antlers too.

## 8. Gladius Maximus
*Lion · People's Champion · Rome, 80 AD · Rare*

Undefeated in the arena, mainly because his opponents get distracted. Gladius has not worn a shirt since boyhood and oils his mane before every bout. He owns a sword and has rarely needed it. He simply flexes at the crowd until the other man gives up and asks for an autograph.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "A big strong lion! Look at his fluffy mane!" · salty: "Gladius Maximus. Undefeated. Mostly by posing." · unhinged: "Shirtless prick oils his mane for an hour and wins by flexing. I hate how well it fucking works."

**Three lost items:** Ceremonial Gladius (*Still has the price tag. Never swung in anger.*) · Single Shoulder Guard (*Covers one shoulder. The other is for the fans.*) · Flask of Mane Oil (*Olive, with a hint of victory.*)

**Set, "Crowd Favourite":** Laurel Wreath, Slightly Tilted, Champion's Leather Harness, Arena Sandals

**Says:** Are you not impressed? / Hold my oil. / This is my good side. Both are. / The crowd adores me. Listen. / Careful with the mane.

**True fact:** A lion's roar can be heard from five miles away.

## 9. Dr. Quackenstein
*Pekin Duck · Galvanic Physician · Black Forest, 1790 · Epic*

The Doctor is certain that enough lightning can fix anything: a cold, a broken heart, death. He has been struck fourteen times while proving it and has called every one 'promising'. The villagers gave up on torches years ago. Now they leave soup at the gate and back away.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "A duck doctor with a lightning stick! Zap!" · salty: "Dr. Quackenstein. Fourteen lightning strikes. Learned nothing." · unhinged: "Mad fucking duck's been hit by lightning fourteen times and thinks it's GOING WELL. Do not shake his hand."

**Three lost items:** Copper Lightning Rod (*Works every time. That is the problem.*) · Scorched Lab Coat (*It was white once. So was he.*) · Jar With Something In It (*It blinked. He says that is normal.*)

**Set, "It's Alive":** Brass Storm Goggles, Rubber Apron, Too Late, Insulated Galoshes

**Says:** It's alive! Probably. / More lightning. / Stand back. Further. / Fourteen is not a pattern. / Science does not apologise.

**True fact:** A duck can sleep with one eye open and half its brain awake.

## 10. Selene Nightbloom
*Black Panther · Jewel Thief · Venice, 1610 · Epic*

Every noble house in Venice has been robbed by Selene, and every one of them has since invited her to dinner, hoping to learn how. She arrives through the front door, in velvet, and leaves through a window nobody knew could open. She has never been caught. She has occasionally been bored.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "A sleek black cat lady! She moves so quietly!" · salty: "Selene Nightbloom. Your vault called. It's empty." · unhinged: "Selene could steal the ring off your goddamn finger while shaking your hand. She did. Check."

**Three lost items:** Silk Grappling Line (*Holds a panther and a chandelier's worth of diamonds.*) · Midnight Velvet Catsuit (*Makes no sound. Neither does she.*) · The Doge's Signet (*He thinks it is in his vault. Bless him.*)

**Set, "Moonless":** Black Lace Bauta Mask, Thief's Bandolier, Soft-Pad Rooftop Boots

**Says:** You'll never see me leave. / Lovely vault. Shame about the lock. / I only take what sparkles. / Bored. Is there a harder one? / Check your pockets, darling.

**True fact:** A black panther is not its own species. It is a leopard or jaguar with a dark coat, and the spots are still there in the right light.

## 11. Unit B33-P
*Robot · Butler, Slightly On Fire · Beyond · Epic*

A household robot from a future that has not happened yet, summoned halfway through serving tea. Unit B33-P is unfailingly polite, extremely well read, and has been slightly on fire since it arrived. It has raised this several times. Everyone agrees something should be done.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "A robot butler! Oh no, is that smoke?" · salty: "Unit B33-P. Polite. Helpful. Mildly ablaze." · unhinged: "The robot's on fucking fire and it keeps APOLOGISING. Somebody get a bucket. It's been a week."

**Three lost items:** Silver Serving Tray (*Deflects blades, arrows and unwanted guests.*) · Riveted Tailcoat Chassis (*Rust-proof. Not, it turns out, fire-proof.*) · Small Persistent Flame (*It has been raised at three meetings.*)

**Set, "Service Model":** Antenna With Bow Tie Attachment, Polished Brass Waistcoat Plate, Quiet-Tread Rubber Feet

**Says:** Tea is served. Mind the flames. / I am mildly on fire. No rush. / How may I be of assistance? / Apologies for the smoke. / All is well. All is not well.

**True fact:** The word 'robot' comes from a 1920 Czech play, and is taken from 'robota', meaning forced labour.

## 12. Pharaoh Tutankhamoose
*Moose · Boy King, Wrong Continent · Egypt, 1330 BC · Legendary*

Nobody in Egypt had ever seen a moose, so they assumed he was a god and gave him the throne. He was nine. He has gone along with it ever since, because the food is excellent. His antlers do not fit through any door in the palace. The doors have been rebuilt. Twice.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "A moose king with golden antlers! So shiny!" · salty: "Pharaoh Tutankhamoose. Divine ruler. Does not fit through doors." · unhinged: "They saw a moose, panicked, and made him a fucking GOD. He's nine feet wide. Nobody's told him."

**Three lost items:** Crook and Flail, Chewed (*Symbols of divine kingship. He thought they were snacks.*) · Gold Broad Collar (*Forty pounds of gold. He has not noticed.*) · Scarab of the Wrong Continent (*Carved in lapis. Shows, for some reason, a pine tree.*)

**Set, "Divine Antler":** Nemes Headcloth, Cut for Antlers, Royal Pleated Kilt, Gilded Hoof Sandals

**Says:** I am a god. Apparently. / Is it always this hot? / Mind the antlers. / Bring me whatever it is I eat here. / I miss snow. I have never seen snow.

**True fact:** A moose sheds its antlers every winter and grows a whole new set by the end of summer.

## 13. President Baldwin Eagleton
*Bald Eagle · Candidate for Everything · A campaign trail, every year · Absurd*

Baldwin Eagleton has been running for president for as long as anyone can remember, of nowhere in particular, against nobody. He has never held office and has given the victory speech nine times. He promises a fish in every pot and has no idea how pots work. His approval rating is, by his own count, tremendous.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "An eagle in a suit! He's waving at everybody!" · salty: "President Eagleton. President of what? Don't ask him." · unhinged: "Pompous feathered windbag's president of jack shit and he will NOT stop shaking my hand."

**Three lost items:** Gavel of Order (*He bangs it to open meetings, close meetings and win arguments.*) · Navy Campaign Suit (*Flag pin included. Flag unspecified.*) · 'I Voted For Me' Button (*The only confirmed vote so far.*)

**Set, "Landslide":** Straw Boater with Ribbon, Rosette-Covered Sash, Podium-Polished Wingtips

**Says:** My fellow arrivals. / A fish in every pot! / No further questions. / I will look into it. I will not. / Tremendous turnout. Look at this crowd.

**True fact:** The fierce scream you hear from eagles in films is usually a red-tailed hawk. A real bald eagle makes a high, chirping whistle.

## 14. Zorp, Destroyer of Tuesdays
*Alien · Definitely a Human · Beyond · Unhinged*

Zorp came to conquer Earth, landed on a Tuesday, and found it so disappointing that he swore to destroy Tuesdays specifically. He wears a trench coat and a hat and insists he is a normal human man named Greg. He is three feet tall and grey. Everyone has agreed, quietly, to play along.

**Smudge announces them:** sweet: "A little grey person in a big coat! Hello, Greg!" · salty: "Zorp. Says he's Greg. He is not Greg." · unhinged: "That's a fucking ALIEN in a trench coat and we're all just calling him Greg?? Fine. Hi, Greg."

**Three lost items:** Ray Gun Disguised as an Umbrella (*It is not disguised well. It hums.*) · Human Trench Coat, Adult Size (*Four feet too long. He calls it 'the fashion'.*) · Earth Calendar, Tuesdays Removed (*Scorched out one by one. He is up to 2041.*)

**Set, "Normal Human Man":** Hat That Hides Nothing, Fake Moustache, Upside Down, Two Left Human Shoes

**Says:** Greetings, fellow humans. / I am Greg. I enjoy bread. / Tuesday must fall. / Do not look under the hat. / Take me to your manager.

**True fact:** In 1977 a radio telescope in Ohio picked up a 72-second signal from space so strong that the astronomer wrote 'Wow!' on the printout. Nobody has explained it for certain.
