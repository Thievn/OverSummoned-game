Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how OverSummoned ("OverSummoned," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the OverSummoned website and game (the "Service"). OverSummoned operates the Service. If you have questions, contact us at support@oversummoned.com.

Information we collect

When you create or use an account, we collect the email address and username you provide. If you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information from Google, including your name, email address, and profile picture.

We collect gameplay data, such as your progress, heroes, inventory, activity in the game, and similar records needed to operate your account.

We also collect basic technical and log data, such as your browser type, device information, IP address, and the pages you visit. We use cookies and local storage to keep you signed in and to help the Service work as expected.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to operate and provide the Service, to create and maintain your account, to keep the Service secure, to respond to support requests, and to improve the game and our site.

We do not sell personal data

We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

We share personal information only with service providers who host and operate the Service for us, and only as needed for that work. Those providers are required to keep the information confidential and to use it only to provide their services to us.

We may also disclose information if we believe we are required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of OverSummoned, our players, or the public.

Google user data

We use information received from Google only to sign you in to your OverSummoned account. We do not sell that information, and we do not share it with other parties except as described in this policy. OverSummoned's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed to operate the Service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We may retain limited records as required by law.

Deleting your information

You may ask us to delete your account and personal information by emailing support@oversummoned.com. We will review and act on requests in a reasonable time, except where we must retain information to comply with the law or to protect the Service.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us at support@oversummoned.com and we will take steps to delete it. This practice is intended to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page and change the effective date. Your continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
