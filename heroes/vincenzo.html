The history behind Vincenzo Moretti

Chicago in 1927 was Al Capone's city, and Prohibition had done the opposite of what was intended. Banning the legal liquor trade handed an enormous untaxed market to anyone willing to break the law, and the profits bought police, judges and elections.

Capone was never convicted of anything to do with the violence. In 1931 the federal government got him on tax evasion — the logic being that income is taxable whether or not you earned it legally. He served most of his sentence at Alcatraz.
