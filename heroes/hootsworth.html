The history behind Inspector Hootsworth

Sherlock Holmes first appeared in print in 1887, in A Study in Scarlet. The famous deerstalker hat is never named in any of the stories. It came from the illustrator, Sidney Paget, who drew Holmes in one for The Strand Magazine, and the picture stuck harder than the text.

Real detectives of the 1880s had far less to work with. Scotland Yard did not open its Fingerprint Bureau until 1901. Before that, identifying a repeat offender meant measuring the lengths of his head, arms and feet and looking him up in a card index.
