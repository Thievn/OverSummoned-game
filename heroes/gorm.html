The history behind Gorm the Mighty

The 1983 toy aisle ran on a rule change. Until 1984 US regulators effectively banned programme-length commercials for children, which kept cartoons and toy lines apart. When the restriction was relaxed, a cartoon could exist purely to sell the figures.

He-Man had arrived in 1982 with the logic reversed: the toys came first, and the fiction was written afterwards to explain them. The figures sold in enormous numbers, and nearly every action-figure cartoon of the decade followed the same order of operations.
