The history behind Raspawtin

Grigori Rasputin was murdered in St Petersburg in December 1916 by a group of nobles who believed his hold over the Tsarina was destroying Russia. The story everyone knows — poisoned cakes and wine that did nothing, several bullets, a beating, and finally drowning — comes largely from the memoirs of one of his killers, Felix Yusupov, who had a book to sell.

The autopsy was less theatrical and more grim: a gunshot to the head, and no evidence of poison in his system. He was dead before he reached the water. The legend of the man who would not die was manufactured almost immediately, and has comfortably outlived the facts.
