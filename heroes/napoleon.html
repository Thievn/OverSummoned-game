The history behind Napoleon Bone-apart

Napoleon Bonaparte was not short. He stood somewhere around 5 foot 6 or 7 in modern measurements, which was unremarkable for a Frenchman of his day. The myth comes partly from French inches being longer than English ones, and mostly from British caricaturists who found a tiny ranting emperor funnier than an average-sized one.

1805 was his best year and his worst. In October the Royal Navy destroyed the combined French and Spanish fleets at Trafalgar, ending any hope of invading Britain. Six weeks later he won at Austerlitz, generally considered his finest battle, by deliberately weakening his own right flank to bait an attack.
