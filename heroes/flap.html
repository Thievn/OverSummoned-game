The history behind Commander Flap

Flash Gordon reached cinemas as a serial in 1936, with Buster Crabbe in the lead and a budget large enough to make it the most expensive serial of its day. Audiences watched a chapter a week, each one ending on a cliffhanger engineered to sell next week's ticket.

Those cliffhangers were frequently cheated. A hero shown plunging to certain death would be revealed the following week to have jumped clear a moment earlier — in footage that had not been in the previous instalment at all. The serial format, and that specific trick, shaped the blockbusters that came decades later.
