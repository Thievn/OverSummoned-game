The word 'robot' comes from a 1920 Czech play, and is taken from 'robota', meaning forced labour.

The history behind Unit B33-P

At the 1939 World's Fair in New York, Westinghouse showed off Elektro, a seven-foot aluminium man who could walk on command, speak about seven hundred recorded words, count on his fingers and smoke a cigarette. He later got a robot dog called Sparko.

The first robot with a real job was far less charming. Unimate, a one-armed machine, started work at a General Motors plant in New Jersey in 1961, lifting hot metal castings off a line so that people no longer had to.
