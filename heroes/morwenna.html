The history behind Morwenna Duskwing

The German witch trials of the 1600s were among the deadliest in Europe. In the prince-bishoprics of Würzburg and Bamberg alone, several hundred people were executed within a few years. Most were women, most were poor, and confessions were extracted under torture that no account can honestly separate from the words produced.

The trials fed on themselves: a confession required naming accomplices, which produced more accusations, which produced more trials. They ended not because anyone was found innocent, but because the machinery eventually reached people powerful enough to stop it.
