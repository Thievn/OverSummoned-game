The history behind Mad Jack Ramsbottom

The Normandy landings of 6 June 1944 put roughly 156,000 Allied troops ashore in a single day, supported by a deception operation that convinced German command the real invasion was coming at Calais — a belief some held for weeks afterwards.

The war also produced Lieutenant Colonel Jack Churchill, a real British officer who carried a longbow, a broadsword and a set of bagpipes into the Second World War, and is credited with the last recorded longbow kill in British military history. His stated view was that any officer going into action without his sword was improperly dressed.
