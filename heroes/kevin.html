The history behind Kevin Latham

The open-plan office was invented to make work more human. The German Bürolandschaft movement of the 1950s proposed scattered, plant-screened desks as an escape from regimented rows.

What crossed the Atlantic was the cheap part. Robert Propst designed the Action Office in 1968 as flexible, height-variable workspace; employers bought the panels, packed them tight and produced the cubicle. Propst spent the rest of his life saying he had not intended that, and called the result monolithic insanity.
