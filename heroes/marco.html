The history behind Marco Reyes

Greyhounds are the fastest dogs alive, capable of around 45 miles per hour, and they sleep roughly eighteen hours a day. They are sprinters, not distance runners: enormous acceleration, a very short tank, and an overwhelming preference for the sofa.

Their build is ancient. Dogs of recognisably greyhound shape appear in Egyptian tomb art thousands of years old, which makes the modern racing dog one of the oldest continuously recognisable animal silhouettes in human art.
