The history behind Pharaoh Tutankhamoose

Tutankhamun became pharaoh at about eight or nine and died at about eighteen or nineteen, around 1323 BC. He was a minor king, and that is why he is famous: his small tomb was overlooked by robbers, and Howard Carter found it nearly intact in 1922.

Among the thousands of objects inside was a dagger with an iron blade, made centuries before Egyptians could smelt iron. In 2016 tests confirmed that the metal came from a meteorite. The Egyptians had a phrase for the material: 'iron from the sky'.
