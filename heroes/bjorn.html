The history behind Bjorn the Twice-Dead

Around the year 890 a Norwegian chieftain named Ohthere visited King Alfred in England and described his home in the far north. Alfred's scribes wrote it down. Ohthere said he had sailed north partly for the walruses, 'because they have very fine ivory in their tusks', and he brought some as a gift.

The cod he would have fished are still caught off Lofoten every winter. Hung on wooden racks, the fish dries in the cold wind into stockfish, which keeps for years. It was one of the first things Norway ever exported.
