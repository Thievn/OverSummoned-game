The history behind Rex Sizzle

Sunset Strip in 1987 was the centre of glam metal. Bands flyered the Strip by hand, played the Whisky and the Roxy, and were signed on the strength of a crowd rather than a demo. Appetite for Destruction came out that year and went on to become one of the best-selling debut albums ever made.

The hair required industrial quantities of aerosol spray, and the scene ended almost overnight. When Nevermind arrived in 1991, labels dropped glam acts within months. Few genres have been replaced that fast.
