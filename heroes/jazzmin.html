The history behind Jazzmin Sweat

The 1980s fitness boom was built on home video. Jane Fonda's Workout, released in 1982, became one of the best-selling home videos of the decade and helped sell VCRs to households that had no other reason to own one.

The look — leotard over tights, leg warmers, headband — came out of dance rehearsal wear, routed through the film Flashdance in 1983. Leg warmers have a real purpose for dancers, keeping muscles warm between takes. Almost nobody wearing them to aerobics class in 1984 needed them.
