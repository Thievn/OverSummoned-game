The history behind Roxy Riot

London punk in 1977 lasted about eighteen months and rearranged British music permanently. The Roxy in Covent Garden ran as a punk venue for roughly a hundred days; the Sex Pistols released Never Mind the Bollocks in October, and shops were prosecuted for displaying the title.

The scene's actual innovation was not the noise but the premise: that you could form a band before you could play. A famous fanzine printed three chord diagrams and the instruction to go and form a band. Most of what followed in British independent music came out of people taking that literally.
