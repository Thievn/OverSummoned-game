The history behind Sgt. Max Carnage

The 1980s action film ran on a small, repeatable formula: an outnumbered hero, a jungle or a tower block, an armoury of implausible weapons, and a one-liner delivered over the corpse. The genre was enormously profitable and almost entirely interchangeable.

The weapons were mostly cinematic fiction. The shoulder-fired miniguns of the era weigh far too much to carry, let alone fire by hand, and need an external power source. Blank-firing props and hidden mounts did the work the biceps got the credit for.
