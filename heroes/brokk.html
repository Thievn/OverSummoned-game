The history behind Brokk

The last Ice Age ended around 11,700 years ago, and for much of it Europe was steppe and tundra rather than forest. Sea levels were low enough that Britain was joined to the continent by a landmass archaeologists call Doggerland, now under the North Sea, where trawlers still occasionally bring up tools and bone.

Ice Age people were not merely surviving. The cave paintings at Chauvet and Lascaux, the carved figurines, the bone flutes — the oldest known instruments are over 40,000 years old. Art is roughly as old as we are.
