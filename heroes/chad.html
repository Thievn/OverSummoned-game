The history behind Chad Hodler

The 2021 crypto cycle ran on a vocabulary invented almost by accident. 'HODL' began as a drunk misspelling of 'hold' in a 2013 forum post by someone explaining why he was not selling, and was retroactively turned into an acronym by people who found the original story insufficiently dignified.

The cycle peaked in November 2021 and fell roughly 75 per cent over the following year. The pattern — a new asset, a wave of certainty, a long retreat — is old enough that the Dutch tulip traders of 1637 would have recognised the shape of it.
