The history behind Mona Lisard

In August 1609 Galileo carried his new telescope up the bell tower in St Mark's Square and let the senators of Venice look through it. They could see ships two hours before they reached port, and they doubled his salary. The next March he published what he had seen in the sky, in Venice.

Venice was also where a painter went shopping. The city had specialist colour-sellers, the vendecolori, who dealt in pigments from all over the trading world, including ultramarine ground from lapis lazuli that cost more than gold.
