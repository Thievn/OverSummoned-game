The history behind Dr. Quackenstein

In the 1780s an Italian doctor, Luigi Galvani, found that a dead frog's leg kicked when he touched it with two different metals. He published in 1791 and called it 'animal electricity'. His nephew took the idea on tour, wiring up the bodies of executed criminals in front of paying crowds.

A teenager named Mary Shelley grew up hearing about those shows. In 1818 she published Frankenstein. The book never says exactly how the creature is brought to life; the lightning and the laboratory came later, from the stage and the films.
