The history behind Dolly LaRue

On the night of 31 May 1942 three Japanese midget submarines slipped into Sydney Harbour. One torpedo missed an American cruiser and sank a converted ferry, killing twenty-one sailors. A week later a submarine surfaced offshore and shelled the eastern suburbs, around Rose Bay and Bondi.

For the rest of the war Bondi Beach was strung with barbed wire and tank traps against an invasion that never came. Sydney filled with servicemen on leave, and the dance halls and canteens stayed open late to keep up with them.
