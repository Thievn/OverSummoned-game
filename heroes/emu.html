The history behind General Emu

In 1932 Australia declared war on birds and lost. Around 20,000 emus had moved into Western Australian farmland, and the government despatched soldiers of the Royal Australian Artillery under Major G. P. W. Meredith with two Lewis machine guns.

The emus scattered into small groups, ran at speeds the gunners could not track, and absorbed hits without going down. After roughly a week the operation was withdrawn, restarted, and abandoned again. Meredith later remarked that if he had a division with the birds' ability to take bullets, it would face any army in the world.
