The history behind Bella Pawsworth

Flamingos are not born pink. They hatch grey and turn pink from carotenoid pigments in the algae and brine shrimp they eat, filtered through a beak held upside down in the water.

Miami's other pink is Art Deco. The South Beach district holds one of the largest concentrations of 1930s Deco architecture anywhere, saved from demolition in the 1970s and 80s by a preservation campaign led largely by Barbara Baer Capitman. The famous pastel paint scheme was applied later, by a designer, and is not original.
