The history behind Jacko Riley

Bondi's surf lifesaving club, founded in 1907, is among the oldest in the world, and the reel, line and belt rescue method developed in Australia saved an enormous number of lives before powered craft.

Kangaroos cannot walk backwards in any practical sense — the tail and the shape of the hind legs make it close to impossible. This is the stated reason a kangaroo appears on the Australian coat of arms: an animal that only goes forward.
