The history behind Astrid Ironhowl

By 870 the Norse were settling Iceland, raiding across the Irish Sea and trading as far as Byzantium. The horned helmet is an invention of the 1800s, largely down to costume design for Wagner's operas; no Viking-age horned helmet has ever been excavated.

They were also unusually clean for the period. Combs, tweezers and ear spoons turn up constantly in Norse graves, and an English chronicler grumbled that the Danes combed their hair daily, bathed on Saturdays and changed their clothes often — and thereby had unfair success with English women.
