The history behind Doug from Accounts

The office cubicle was meant to set workers free. Robert Propst designed the Action Office for Herman Miller in the 1960s as a flexible, open workspace. Companies took the cheap part of the idea, the partitions, and packed people in. Propst spent his later years calling the result 'monolithic insanity'.

Capybaras, meanwhile, became famous for being unbothered. In 1982 a keeper at Izu Shaboten Zoo in Japan noticed them crowding into a puddle of warm water, and the zoo has given them a hot spring bath every winter since.
