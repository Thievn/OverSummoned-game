The history behind Pixel Pam

The North American video game crash of 1983 nearly killed the industry. The market was flooded with poor titles on the Atari 2600, retailers lost confidence, and revenues collapsed by something like 97 per cent over two years.

The emblem of it was a landfill in Alamogordo, New Mexico, where Atari buried truckloads of unsold cartridges, including the notoriously rushed E.T. game. It was treated as an urban legend for thirty years until a 2014 excavation dug the cartridges up and proved it true.
