The history behind President Baldwin Eagleton

The bald eagle has been on the Great Seal of the United States since 1782. Benjamin Franklin was not a fan. In a letter to his daughter two years later he called it 'a bird of bad moral character' that steals fish from other birds, and said the turkey was 'a much more respectable bird'. He never proposed the turkey officially.

The longest inaugural address belongs to William Henry Harrison, who in 1841 spoke for nearly two hours in the cold, about 8,400 words. He died a month later, the shortest presidency in American history.
