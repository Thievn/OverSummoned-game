The history behind Cleocatra

Cleopatra VII ruled Egypt from 51 to 30 BC, and almost nothing people picture about her is Egyptian. Her family, the Ptolemies, were Macedonian Greeks who had held the throne for roughly three centuries and mostly never bothered to learn the local language. Cleopatra did, and was reportedly fluent in a string of others besides.

She also sits oddly in time. The Great Pyramid of Giza was already about two and a half thousand years old when she was born — meaning she lived closer to the invention of the pizza and the Moon landing than to the building of the pyramid she would have seen from her own kingdom.
