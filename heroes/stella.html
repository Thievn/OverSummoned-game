The history behind Captain Stella Comet

The future as imagined in 1953 was all rockets and rayguns and almost no computers. Fins, bubble helmets, and a crew who navigated by eye — because the idea that a machine might do the arithmetic had not yet taken hold in popular fiction.

Real 1953 was a strange year to guess from. The structure of DNA was published that April, and the first ascent of Everest was reported in London on the morning of the coronation. Space travel was still four years from Sputnik.
