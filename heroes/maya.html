The history behind Maya Washington

The golden retriever was created in the Scottish Highlands in the second half of the 1800s by Dudley Marjoribanks, Lord Tweedmouth, who kept meticulous stud books. Every golden retriever alive descends from that programme.

They were bred to carry shot game back undamaged, which required a dog with a soft mouth and an unusual willingness to hand things over. The retrieving instinct that makes them tireless about a thrown ball is the same trait, with nothing left to retrieve.
